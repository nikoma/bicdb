# BicDB 1.0.322-beta

Phase 5 ships the industry-neutral, cell-scoped HA and recovery boundary.

## Added

- New `bicdb-cell-ha` crate with no application, SQL, fleet, key-provider, or
  database-selector authority.
- Separate Lease, Recovery, and Auditor trust roles with threshold signatures
  and one-role-per-key enforcement.
- Quorum-certified writer epochs and short-lived replica leases with exact
  Cell/group/replica/key/manifest scope.
- Kernel-exclusive, process-lifetime Cell-volume lease.
- Database commit admission fencing and a post-WAL, fsynced durable-sequence
  witness.
- Graceful old-primary fence receipts and expiry/skew-based non-overlapping
  promotion checks.
- Source-signed, Cell-encrypted, destination- and epoch-bound native commit
  replication with contiguous anti-replay state.
- Full encrypted backup candidates, one-time archive keys wrapped under the
  Cell Backup purpose, Recovery-quorum lineage certification, and short-lived
  restore authorization.
- Inert new-directory restore and exact accepted-history binding; restore
  cannot overwrite or promote an active database.
- Keyless failover sequencing through fence, durable selection, activation,
  route publication, old-primary rebuild, and completion.
- Auditor-certified topology RPO/RTO and restore-drill evidence.
- Phase-5 CLI flags and `BICDB_CELL_HA_*` environment mappings.

## Correctness and security findings closed during integration

- Phase 5 now inherits Phase-4 fleet verification instead of being rejected by
  a stale exact-Phase-4 constructor check.
- Replica-local manifests bind one shared canonical HA configuration without
  requiring distinct volume and replica identities to hash identically.
- A backup retains the exact archive cut even if the live durable watermark
  advances before its manifest is assembled.
- Backup-head and replication-witness transitions are atomic against racing
  callers.
- Lease replacement is one compare-and-swap critical section.
- HA witness publication uses unique crash-safe staging files rather than a
  fixed stale temporary name.
- Backup passphrases are redacted from `Debug` and zeroized on drop.
- Unsupported synchronous-quorum topology is rejected instead of advertising
  a false zero-commit RPO over asynchronous replication.
- Asynchronous topology records a declared commit RPO and Auditor-measured
  drill result without claiming continuous commit-time RPO enforcement.

## Verification

- Exact public `CellRuntime::open` path with Phase-3 application, Phase-4
  fleet authorization, Phase-5 HA, pre-key wrong-replica-key refusal, real
  commit fence, and durable witness.
- Real encrypted Primary and Standby stores with signed/encrypted commit
  replication.
- Graceful Primary fence, threshold-certified next epoch, certified backup,
  Recovery-only authorized restore, and post-fence write refusal.
- Cross-replica signature forgery, wrong destination, stale epoch, insufficient
  quorum, rollback, and unsafe topology refusal.
- Kernel volume-lock exclusivity and crash release.

Regulated-data production admission remains explicitly denied.
