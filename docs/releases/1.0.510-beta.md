# 1.0.510-beta: recovery collection workers release their range context on teardown

Three range-recovery collection rounds (abort promises, ready quorum and
completion quorum) cancelled their workers on return but did not wait for
them. Each worker holds the operation's range context, so the next recovery
step on the same range could be refused as busy although the previous step had
returned. This is the fault fixed for the observation round in 1.0.500-beta.

- The three rounds now cancel their workers and wait up to 250 ms for them to
  leave before returning. A worker still inside a peer exchange after that
  bound is left running and keeps its context.
- The observation round uses the same shared owner; its behaviour is unchanged.
- In the strong-read path, the receiver and candidate workers are awaited once
  their reply has arrived, closing a short window in which the caller returned
  while the context was still held.

Limits:

- The strong-read probe workers are deliberately not awaited: a stalled
  minority must not delay the majority verdict.
- With a peer stuck mid-exchange, an abort, ready or completion result can
  arrive up to 250 ms later than before.
- Only the shared owner has unit tests; the original intermittent busy refusal
  is a race and is not reproduced by a test.
