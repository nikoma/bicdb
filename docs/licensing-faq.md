BicDB is licensed under the [Apache License, Version 2.0](../LICENSE)
(`Apache-2.0`), unmodified and OSI-approved. The license text governs if this
summary differs from it.

## What you may do

Apache-2.0 lets you use, copy, modify, distribute and sublicense BicDB, in source
or object form, for any purpose, including commercial use. That covers
proprietary applications, SaaS of any size, embedding BicDB in a product, hosting
BicDB for others, selling support, and publishing forks. There are no revenue,
user, tenant or instance limits, and no separate commercial license is needed.
The license also carries an express patent grant from contributors (section 3).

## What you must do when you redistribute

When you distribute BicDB or a derivative work (section 4):

- give recipients a copy of the [LICENSE](../LICENSE);
- keep the attribution notices in [NOTICE](../NOTICE) in the copies you
  distribute (a NOTICE file, the documentation, or a display the software makes);
- mark files you modified with a prominent change notice;
- keep existing copyright, patent, trademark and attribution notices in the source.

Your own modifications and the larger work may carry terms of your choice, as long
as your use of BicDB itself complies with the license. Private modifications need
not be published.

## What the license does not grant

The license does not grant permission to use the BicDB or WalkNorth names, logos
or trademarks, except as needed to describe the origin of the work and to
reproduce the NOTICE file (section 6). BicDB is provided "AS IS", without
warranties (sections 7 and 8).

## Third-party components

Dependencies and vendored components keep their own licenses. They are listed in
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md); binary distributors must
reproduce the notices required by the exact components they ship.

## Earlier versions

Source versions before 1.0.523-beta were distributed under the custom BicDB
License 1.0 (Apache 2.0 with additional restrictions). The
[transition record](licensing-transition.md) keeps that history.
