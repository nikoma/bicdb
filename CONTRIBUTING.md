# Contributing to BicDB

Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for repository completion and build instructions.
Keep changes focused, preserve dependency notices and source provenance, and run
checks appropriate to the affected files. Do not submit confidential code or
material you lack permission to contribute. Identify third-party/generated inputs,
their source revision and license, and any changes. AI assistance does not replace
human responsibility to review provenance and correctness.

## Licensing transition and inbound rights

The covered engine now uses [BicDB License 1.0](LICENSE), with the
separately licensed boundaries in [LICENSE-SCOPE.md](LICENSE-SCOPE.md). A submission
must state the contributor's actual inbound permissions for the affected component.
Do not presume a commit or pull request establishes broad commercial relicensing
rights. Apache section 5 supplies the default inbound terms as incorporated in the
complete BicDB License. Apache section 4 permits different terms for your own
modifications; no same-license requirement is imposed. Existing third-party and
previously granted permissions remain.
A DCO sign-off certifies the matters it states; it is not automatically a broad
commercial sublicensing/relicensing agreement or an assignment of copyright.

The [prospective contributor agreement draft](licensing/contributor-agreement.draft.md)
and its adoption proposal are **not active**. Nobody is marked as having accepted
it. Contributors would retain ownership and give the identified authorized project
recipient the stated copyright/patent permissions. The owner must identify that
recipient and establish actual assent before relying on it for future submissions.
Existing contributors' rights are not changed by this page.

Until that process is adopted, record the actual inbound basis per accepted
contribution; do not represent new contributions as carrying broader commercial
relicensing rights merely because they were merged. Third-party code is always
separately identified and cannot be swept into a blanket rights assertion.
