# BicDB 1.0.13-beta

Release date: 2026-08-03

## Quorum resolution of old-leader tails

- Moves leadership recovery into the cluster supervisor, outside SQL commit
  locks, and keeps each range read-only until its exact current epoch recovers.
- Probes old-leader tail indexes on authenticated current voters and validates
  node, range, epoch, index, durable progress, and command checksum identity.
- Adopts and physically applies a command when a certificate, applied record,
  or voter quorum proves the decision.
- Aborts prepared or minority-provisional commands only when the observed
  quorum proves that unavailable voters could not have completed a decision.
- Fails closed on ambiguous outcomes, divergent command identities, mixed
  certified-commit/abort evidence, compaction gaps, and unavailable quorum.
- Recovers at most 16 local-leader ranges per supervisor tick with a fair
  rotating cursor and non-blocking reservations; one range cannot starve or
  pause recovery and metadata supervision for independent ranges.
- Bounds tail work by the existing repair command/batch limits and repairs
  reachable followers after every resolved outcome.
- Extends authenticated cluster data RPC to protocol v10 with point probes and
  adds commit, abort, ambiguity, divergence, and TCP regression coverage.
