# Prospective BicDB contributor agreement — draft, not active

Unresolved execution fields: authorized project recipient and notice channel;
contributor legal identity and authority; covered submissions; assent record.
No person has accepted this draft merely by committing, opening a PR, signing a
DCO, or having this document merged. It does not apply retroactively.

## Proposed agreement

1. **Parties and contributions.** “Project Recipient” means the specifically
   identified party authorized to receive rights for BicDB, entered in the
   executed agreement. “Contributor” means the executing rights holder or its
   authorized representative. “Contribution” means only material intentionally
   submitted and identified for inclusion in BicDB under this executed agreement.
   Material marked not a contribution and disclosed third-party material are excluded.
2. **Ownership.** Contributor retains ownership. This agreement transfers no title.
   Contributor identifies third-party portions and limitations and represents only
   that it has authority to give the permissions it expressly grants, including
   necessary employer/client authorization for its own contribution.
3. **Copyright permission.** Subject to applicable law, Contributor grants Project
   Recipient a perpetual, worldwide, nonexclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable license
   to reproduce, modify, prepare derivatives, display, perform, use, distribute and
   sublicense its Contribution in source/object form. Project Recipient may offer
   it and derivatives under the BicDB community license and separate commercial or
   other licenses, including proprietary terms, and sublicense these rights through
   multiple tiers. This permission binds no third-party rights Contributor cannot
   license and does not revoke any earlier downstream grant.
4. **Patent permission.** To the extent Contributor owns or is authorized to license
   them, Contributor grants Project Recipient a perpetual, worldwide, nonexclusive,
   royalty-free license, with the sublicensing rights needed for community and
   commercial distribution, to make, have made, use, offer, sell, import and transfer
   the Contribution and its combination with BicDB as submitted, limited to patent
   claims necessarily infringed by that Contribution or combination. No patent is
   asserted to exist and no rights to unauthorizing third-party patents are granted.
   Counsel should settle a proportionate patent-claim termination provision before
   execution; this draft does not silently import another license's termination rule.
5. **Provenance and notification.** Contributor identifies included third-party
   material and applicable notices. Contributor will notify Project Recipient if
   it learns that its authority representation was materially inaccurate. Submission
   does not require Contributor to provide future work or support.
6. **No additional warranty.** Except for the authority representation, the
   Contribution is supplied as is to the extent law permits, without promises of
   performance, merchantability, fitness, or non-infringement. No indemnity, governing
   jurisdiction, arbitration requirement, or legal address is invented here.
7. **Acceptance and version.** The parties must record affirmative assent, agreement
   version, contribution scope and date of actual execution. New agreement versions
   do not automatically replace executed agreements. Project Recipient may decline
   a submission; nothing requires its incorporation or grants trademark endorsement.

## Adoption proposal

- Have the owner identify the authorized Project Recipient and confirm that the
  copyright/patent sublicensing permissions match the intended commercial program.
  Resolve the limited patent-termination and local enforceability wording before signing.
- Use an explicit individual or corporate assent record for prospective submissions,
  with employer authorization where needed. Store sensitive evidence privately and
  associate an acceptance reference with the actual submitted revisions.
- Then update CONTRIBUTING.md to describe the adopted version and actual process.
  A PR checklist may ask for the acceptance reference but must not manufacture assent.
  No external CLA service, contributor messaging or acceptance automation is installed.
- Inventory prior contributions separately. Seek only specifically necessary rights
  or retain underlying licenses; do not imply blanket retroactive consent. Apache's
  permissions for derivatives as a whole and continuing underlying grants must both
  be respected. No contributor is required to assign ownership under this proposal.
