# BicDB 1.0.37-beta

Release date: 2026-08-03

## Fresh post-repair bucket verification

- Re-exports each repaired bucket from the destination after the final apply;
  pre-repair destination files can never satisfy verification.
- Stages fresh checksummed frames under the repair run's existing file, step,
  work, and total disk-byte bounds.
- Compares certified-source and fresh-destination streams exactly with bounded
  memory and cursor-checkpointed resumability.
- Fails closed on missing, extra, reordered, or changed records and reaches
  `verified` only after both fresh streams end together.
- Loads 1.0.36 repair checkpoints without discarding their immutable source,
  destination, or mutation-batch artifacts.
- Keeps the range fence active: per-bucket verification is not yet the
  range-level certificate required to declare all divergent voters healthy.
