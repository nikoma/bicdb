# BicDB commercial licensing

**Apache 2.0 + three exceptions.** The [BicDB License](../LICENSE) permits commercial
applications, proprietary embedding, unlimited application SaaS, private modifications,
consulting, support and community forks.

A separate written commercial license is needed to:

1. Supply BicDB or a derivative as a general-purpose hosted/managed database service.
2. Commercially supply it primarily as a standalone database engine, appliance or
   platform, including OEM/white-label products, renamed versions and thin wrappers.
3. Remove BicDB origin attribution from its included administration console when
   redistributing it for a white-label database offering.

The [scenario guide](licensing-faq.md) explains the application-versus-database
boundary. The complete license governs. An agreement overrides the exceptions only
within its stated scope; third-party rights and credits remain independently required.

## Request commercial authorization

Open a [commercial licensing inquiry](https://github.com/nikoma/bicdb/issues/new?title=Commercial%20licensing%20inquiry)
in this repository to contact the maintainers. Describe the intended database
product, hosting service, or console distribution at a high level. The issue is
public: keep confidential business information and credentials out of it and ask
for a private follow-up channel if needed.

The authorized licensors and covered scope are identified in
[LICENSE-SCOPE.md](../LICENSE-SCOPE.md). An inquiry, repository access, or payment
for support does not itself grant reserved rights. Authorization requires a
separate written agreement from the relevant authorized rights holder.
