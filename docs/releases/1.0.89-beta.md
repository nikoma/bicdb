# BicDB 1.0.89-beta

This release hardens the two maintenance features shipped in 1.0.86/1.0.87
against the crash and concurrency windows a production reviewer flagged. It
changes no public schema or report fields; the fixes are to durability,
boundedness, and test coverage.

## Status spill: copy-on-write publication

The 1.0.86 spill rewrote its authoritative pages in place, synced them, and
only afterwards published the descriptor that names them. A crash in that
window left the old descriptor pointing at modified pages, and reopen refused
the store with an entry-count mismatch. A concurrent reader could likewise
select a page from the old range map and read it after a checkpoint had
rewritten it, turning a committed outcome into a miss.

The spill is now copy-on-write:

- `persist` writes the merged outcome set to freshly allocated pages and syncs
  them, then atomically swaps the in-memory chain. It never mutates a page a
  reader can reach.
- The pages the swap retires stay on disk, untouched, and are only returned to
  the free list by `reclaim_retired`, which the checkpoint calls after it has
  made the descriptor naming the new chain durable. Every crash window now
  leaves either the old descriptor with the old (unmodified) pages or the new
  descriptor with the new (already synced) pages — never a descriptor pointing
  at a page changed out from under it.
- `lookup` holds the chain lock across the whole page read, so a concurrent
  `persist` cannot swap the chain and reclaim a page mid-read. Demand reads
  serialize; they are rare (only outcomes above a pinned watermark).

## Orphaned-free-page reclamation: strict envelopes

- The byte budget is now strict: the next page must fit in the remaining
  bytes, mirroring vacuum. The old `examined >= max` check let a limit of
  `page_size + 1` admit two pages.
- `max_free_list_visits` gains a hard ceiling (`MAX_FREE_RECLAIM_FREE_LIST_VISITS`,
  4 Mi visits / 32 MiB membership set) instead of being unbounded above.
- The step checks its deadline after the prerequisite pool flush and free-list
  walk and yields rather than starting the scan once it is exhausted.
- `free_list_members`'s contract is corrected: a non-Free member fails as
  corruption, but a cycle is not separately detected — it revisits until the
  visit budget is spent and the walk defers, so a cyclic list surfaces as "too
  large" rather than spinning.

## Tests added

- `an_interrupted_spill_persist_leaves_the_store_recoverable`: fault-injects a
  write failure mid-publish of a new spill chain and asserts the checkpoint
  fails without corrupting the prior chain and that restart recovers every
  committed row.
- `concurrent_readers_and_spill_checkpoints_stay_consistent`: readers resolve
  spilled outcomes while checkpoints republish the chain underneath them.
- `orphan_reclaim_defers_when_the_free_list_is_too_large` now builds a real
  oversized free list and a stranded orphan, asserts the deferral mutates
  nothing, and that a fitting budget reclaims the orphan.

## Explicit boundary

The spill still materializes the merged outcome set for one checkpoint
(`O(spill)` temporary memory during the persist, not during recovery or
lookup). Retired pages are freed only after the replacing descriptor is
durable, so a crash between a persist and its reclaim leaks at most one
generation of pages — detectable as orphaned allocated pages, not corruption.
